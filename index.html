Research Experience

  1. Animal-skin-pattern-inspired multifunctional composites by generative AI click here

    May 2024 — December 2024

    Bioinspired composite materials offer unique advantages by replicating the structural properties of their natural counterparts. However, their complex hierarchical architecture, combined with a limited number of observations, makes it challenging to capture all structural features and modify them for functional optimization without losing their inherent characteristics. To address this, we applied Generative Artificial Intelligence (GenAI) to design composites inspired by animal skin patterns, utilizing a small dataset to generate diverse configurations that closely resemble natural designs. Through computational simulations, we explored the structure-mechanics relationship, uncovering notable variations in mechanical properties and identifying patterns with superior performance. We validated these optimized configurations through tensile tests on specimens fabricated using multimaterial 3D printing. This study highlights GenAI's role in enhancing structural design, enabling rational bioinspired innovations, and is complemented by an educational web page featuring interactive games for public engagement.
